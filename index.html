The person behind the vision

Built on a Vision of Exceptional Hospitality.

Madam Usiju Founder

Usiju World Events was established from a vision to make event planning and hospitality more convenient for people.

The idea was to bring essential event services together, reducing the need for customers to source their venue, decoration, catering and equipment from different providers.

Over time, the business developed into an events and hospitality destination, with its founder, Madam Usiju, remaining involved in consultations and event details.
