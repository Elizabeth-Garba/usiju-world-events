### More Than a Venue. A Destination for Every Occasion.

At Usiju World Events, we believe every occasion deserves a setting that brings people together beautifully. Located in Jos, Plateau State, we offer an environment designed to make celebrations, gatherings and special moments more enjoyable.

From the venue to the finer details of event preparation, our goal is to make every experience comfortable, seamless and memorable.

## 3. Services

### Exceptional Spaces. Complete Event Solutions.

**Venue Hire**  
 A spacious event hall in a quiet part of Jos, with parking, air conditioning, round-the-clock power, and room for up to 2,000 guests. Suitable for weddings, parties, concerts, conferences, and church programmes.

**Event Planning**  
 Consultations and full coordination for your occasion — from guest flow and timing to working with vendors. The team helps you plan so the day runs without last-minute scrambling.

**Decor & Styling**  
 Indoor and outdoor decoration for weddings, birthdays, corporate events, and more. Themed setups, floral arrangements, and stage styling to match what you have in mind.

**Catering & Rentals**  
 On-site catering for your event, plus rental and sale of chairs, decor items, and kitchen equipment. One stop instead of calling three different suppliers.

## 7. Founder Story

### Built on a Vision of Exceptional Hospitality.

**The Story Behind Usiju**

Usiju World Events was established from a vision to make event planning and hospitality more convenient for people.

The idea was to bring essential event services together, reducing the need for customers to source their venue, decoration, catering and equipment from different providers.

Over time, the business developed into an events and hospitality destination, with its founder, Madam Usiju, remaining involved in consultations and event details.
